# Breaking Changes

This repository's v1.0.0 is its first release; there are no external dependents yet, and the breaking-changes list is empty.

**Future breaking changes** (recorded here):
- Evidence-grading standard adjustments (changes to the A/B/C/D definitions);
- Mechanism-numbering changes (L0–L4 / G0–G4 / S0–S3 / E1–E5);
- Terminology redefinitions (No. 1 / No. 2 / No. 3, etc.).

**Principle**: once mechanism numbers and open-problem numbers are published, they are not reordered or reused.
