# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

Provide a friendly, safe, and inclusive collaborative environment for everyone, regardless of identity, experience, or professional background.

## Standards

**Positive behavior**:
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences;
- Constructive feedback, focused on evidence rather than persons;
- Acknowledge mistakes and accept correction.

**Unacceptable**:
- Harassment, discriminatory remarks, or personal attacks;
- Publishing others' private information;
- Misconduct of any form.

## AI-Generated Content Labeling

Part of this project's early content was generated by multiple AI systems and underwent human review. When contributors submit AI-assisted content, they should label "AI-assisted, human-verified" in the PR description.

## Enforcement

Maintainers (see `MAINTAINERS.md`) are responsible for enforcing this code. Violations may result in warnings, temporary or permanent removal from the project.
