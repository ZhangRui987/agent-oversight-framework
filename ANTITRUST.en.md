# Antitrust Statement

Chapter 12 of this framework (`spec/12-enforcement.md`) discusses enforcement anchors such as "industry admission" and "regulatory compliance". These discussions constitute **policy research and standards recommendations**, and do not form any kind of industry collusion, exclusivity agreement, or market-access cartel.

This project's position:

1. **Non-exclusive**: this framework encourages all implementations and alternatives to coexist and does not restrict any organization from integrating or distributing.
2. **Non-discriminatory**: any standards-compliant implementation, regardless of vendor, scale, or business model, may declare compliance.
3. **Non-mandatory**: this framework's "admission" and "certification" recommendations are optional best-practice references only and do not constitute mandatory obligations.
4. **Openly developed**: all standards and specification evolution follows the public decision process in `GOVERNANCE.md`, in which any interested party may participate.

If you have doubts about whether this repository's content raises antitrust issues, contact the maintainer through the channels in `SECURITY.md`.
