## Why the split

**Documentation uses CC BY-SA 4.0**: copying, translation, and adaptation remain free, and derivative works carry the same license, preventing the governance framework from being closed-sourced and privatized.

**Code uses Apache-2.0**: provides implementers with explicit patent grants. MIT is silent on patents, which is why MIT was not chosen.
