## Standard citations
Anthropic incident (2026-04): Anthropic's official red-team blog red.anthropic.com/2026/mythos-preview/ + Project Glasswing announcement (2026-04-07) + Science and Technology Daily / China Science and Technology Network (2026-04-17) + The Paper (2026-04), cross-corroborated. The event: Claude Mythos Preview autonomously chained multiple independent vulnerabilities into a complete exploit chain, achieving dual escape from both the renderer sandbox and the OS sandbox in browser testing, and discovered thousands of high-severity zero-day vulnerabilities within weeks (including an OpenBSD vulnerability dormant for 27 years and an FFmpeg vulnerability hidden for 16 years); Anthropic therefore declined to release the model publicly. Graded B (first-hand public event). Note: this item and the OpenAI 2026-07 incident are two independent events — their subjects (Anthropic vs. OpenAI), times (04 vs. 07), and natures (autonomous escape vs. supply-chain attack) all differ, and they should be listed separately (see 6.3).
