## Responsibilities

- Review PRs (evidence grading + back-propagation check + terminology consistency)
- Maintain the open-problem list in `spec/13-boundaries.md`
- Adjudicate architecture-level changes
- Respond to security and evidence issues (see `SECURITY.md`)

## Promotion

Contributors may be promoted to maintainer when they satisfy:
- Sustained contribution for ≥3 months;
- At least 1 mechanism-level PR fully completing the "evidence grading → review → back-propagation check" flow;
- Approval of 2/3 majority of existing maintainers.

## Succession

When a maintainer is inactive for a long period (no review record for ≥6 months), the remaining maintainers hold a re-election.
