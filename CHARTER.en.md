# Project Charter

## Mission

Provide an open, evidence-driven governance-architecture design specification for the execution, oversight, and accountability of AI agents, expanding the human-delegable boundary.

## Scope

- **Does**: governance-architecture design, evidence grading, mechanism traceability, standards recommendations.
- **Does not**: sandbox / monitoring-tool implementation (left to mature open-source projects), compliance-consulting services, commercial products.

## Decision Principles

1. **Evidence first**: every mechanism must be traceable to a real lesson (A/B/C/D grading).
2. **Honest boundary**: open problems must be explicitly registered, without glossing over them.
3. **Governance of governance itself**: any revision must pass the back-propagation checklist in `spec/13-boundaries.md`.

## Non-Goals

- Does not pursue "how many violations were caught" as a success metric; success = expansion of the delegable boundary.
- Does not promise "completion" — this project is in maintenance state.
